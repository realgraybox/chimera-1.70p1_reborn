Local change to the bundled libtiff 3.7.0

File:   tiff-3.7.0/libtiff/tif_fax3.h, line 88
Change: TIFFFaxTabEnt.Param changed from uint32 to unsigned short, and the
        struct marked __attribute__((packed)).
Why:    halves the size of each entry in the fax decoding tables, which
        makes the statically linked xvl smaller. The run lengths stored in
        Param are at most 2560, so they fit in 16 bits.
